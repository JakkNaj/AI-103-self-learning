Choose the abstraction boundary

RequirementBoundary
Coordinate projects, agents, models, tools and evaluationMicrosoft Foundry resource/project
Send supported inference requestsCorresponding inference API/client
Retrieve chunks with keyword/vector queriesSearch index and query client/tool
Execute an external business operationAuthenticated function/OpenAPI/MCP integration
Remember dialogue during one interactionConversation/session state
Remember a user's preference across sessionsIsolated durable memory

The product organizing the solution and the service executing one step can both be present. A Foundry resource does not replace an index, transcription operation, or caller's RBAC permissions.

Choose from measured constraints

  1. Required modality and operation.
  2. Quality on representative examples.
  3. Latency, memory, throughput and cost targets.
  4. Processing geography and networking.
  5. Supported model/API version and lifecycle.

Use the smallest adequate model; route harder tasks to a stronger model when the workload permits. Benchmarks help shortlist; your own evaluation establishes suitability.

Compare related questions together

  • Small vs large vs multimodal: “short stable labels” differs from “deep multistep reasoning” and “image plus text.”
  • Vision vs Speech: image evidence differs from audio recognition, synthesized speech and a bidirectional voice session.
  • Document Intelligence vs Content Understanding: known document structure/schema differs from varied multimodal analyzer extraction.
  • RAG vs memory: current governed facts differ from a user's durable preference.
  • Project client vs inference client: managing project capabilities differs from sending a model request.

Study by decision

  • Small vs large vs multimodal models — 22 questions. Choose the smallest model that meets measured quality and capability requirements. Complex reasoning may justify an LLM; image input requires explicit multimodal support.
  • Choose the Azure service — 7 questions. Match input and output: Language analyzes text; Speech recognizes/synthesizes audio; Vision/multimodal models analyze images; Search retrieves; Document Intelligence extracts document structure/fields; Content Understanding applies reusable multimodal schemas.
  • Choose the SDK or client — 1 questions. AIProjectClient accesses supported project capabilities. An OpenAI client sends inference requests. Use the service-specific client for Speech, Language, Search or document analysis.
  • Route easy and difficult requests — 10 questions. Route simple tasks to a validated cheaper model and difficult tasks to a stronger model; retain quality checks and escalation.
